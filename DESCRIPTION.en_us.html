Faculty Glyphic Kannada brings the carved, glyphic character of Faculty Glyphic to the Kannada script, following the Latin's x-height, stroke weight and contrast so both scripts read as one family.

The Latin, Faculty Glyphic, was designed by Dylan Young at Koto Studio in 2024. The Kannada was adapted by Laxmi Mahajan in 2026 from Noto Sans Kannada.

To contribute, see github.com/laxmispace/FacultyGlyphicKannada.
